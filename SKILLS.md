# Capabilities

## Supported work

- Build and refactor Angular components and routes.
- Maintain typed team and player data models.
- Add deterministic tests and accessibility checks.
- Evaluate data-loading and visualization trade-offs.
- Document architecture, provenance, and prototype findings.

## Limitations

- Do not redefine the product goal without a recorded decision.
- Do not introduce a live external data source, tracking, or credentials by
  convenience.
- Do not treat bundled third-party material as reusable PIOS Knowledge.
- Do not promote prototype behavior as a stable service contract.
